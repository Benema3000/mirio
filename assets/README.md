# Menu assets

`menu-adventure.png`, `menu-kart.png`, `menu-sky.png` and `menu-ribbon.png`
are direct 800 × 450 screenshots of this game's rendered scenes, with the HUD
hidden. They show the actual playable levels. The original files in `img/` and
the code that builds Miro's characters and rocket were not altered.

`mirio-logo.png` is a new 2172 × 724 RGBA title wordmark, made on 2026-09-27
with the built-in ImageGen tool and copied into this repository with its alpha
channel intact. No child drawing was supplied as a reference or edited.

Generation prompt:

Create one beautifully polished, compact, horizontal wordmark with the exact text "MIRIO" (M I R I O), and no other words. Joyful, memorable custom chunky rounded lettering, energetic slightly uneven baseline, tactile painted-toy finish and subtle sculpted bevels. Warm ivory letter faces with small golden highlights, deep teal extrusion and a clean dark midnight-blue outer keyline, giving excellent readability on both bright sky and dark space backgrounds. A tiny warm gold four-point sparkle above the final letters and a restrained curved orbital swoosh integrated behind the word can suggest playful planet adventures. Strong simple silhouette, charming premium family-game title design, not a corporate logo. The name must read MIRIO, clearly and accurately, with five letters, no missing or extra letters. Original design: no existing franchise wordmark, no character, no hat, no mascot, no redrawing of a child's artwork. Front-facing with gentle 3D depth, centered, wide roughly 3:1 composition and tight sensible transparent margins. Actual alpha transparency, no backdrop, no checkerboard, no white rectangle, no mockup, no watermark.
